# AGENTS.md

How this demo is built, for anyone (person or agent) working on it.

## The page

One page, `index.html`. The first screen shows the name of a late-night radio show, Small Hours, in the amber of the ground's glow (`rgb(var(--glow))`), with how the page works on the same baseline. Then one section per variation, `#v01` onwards. Each section has a small label over a statement: a few lines of text set large and tight, a caller to the show, with words that open hover cards (triggers). Every variation is a different look and motion for the cards.

`js/index.js` wires the page:

- **Smooth scroll**: ScrollSmoother (`smooth: 1`), off with reduced motion.
- **Frame**: the title, links and tags down the left, the variation numbers in the centre; on the live site the Codrops ad (`#cdawrap`) lands top right, out of the flow on desktop so nothing moves when it arrives.
- **Bar of numbers**: `.pager`, a copy of the frame's variation links fixed at the top centre once the frame has scrolled away, with the current section marked (`aria-current`), on a light pane of frosted glass.
- **Hint**: on touch screens it says to tap the words.
- **Cards**: set up once the fonts are in (`document.fonts.ready`), since the looks measure the text.

## Hover cards

`js/hoverCards.js` runs every card on the page:

- A trigger is a link, `<a class="term" href="#card-id" aria-describedby="card-id">`, so it's reachable with the keyboard and its card is read as its description. The card is a `.card` with `role="tooltip"` in the block's `.cards`, outside the paragraph.
- A card opens on hover, on keyboard focus, and with a tap on touch screens (a second tap closes it). It closes when the pointer leaves the trigger (after 0.1 s, to cross to the next one without flicker), on blur, on Escape, or on a tap anywhere else. One card is open at a time. The listeners sit on the document, one set for every trigger on the page.
- It's placed against its `.block` (the card's offset parent): centred over its trigger, 15px above it, or 15px below when there isn't room above, or on whichever side has more room when neither has enough, and inside the page margins. A look with `place: 'beside'` has its card to the right of the paragraph instead, 60px from it and centred on the trigger's line, wherever there's room for it, so not on phones. A look with `place: 'around'` has its card centred on the trigger both ways, inside the page margins only if it fits there (a ring wider than a phone stays centred on its word). A look with `place: 'over'` has it centred on the trigger too, but moved back inside the page margins, its section and the screen wherever it would run out. A look with `place: 'cursor'` places its card itself, at the pointer; `open()`'s context then holds `pointer`, where the pointer was when the card opened (`{x, y}` on the screen), or `null` when the keyboard or a tap opened it. A look with `place: 'below'` is placed as usual, but tries below its trigger first. A look with `place: 'self'` places its card itself, in `open()`.
- A card stays on screen (below the bar of numbers once it shows) and inside its section, 15px from the lines between sections; where it can't be both, it stays on screen, since a tooltip has to be seen first. The cards that surround or underline their trigger (03's ring, 04's strip) stay centred on it, so near the screen's edges they run past them. Only `left` and `top` are set, plus `transformOrigin`, and `data-side` (`top`, `bottom`, `beside`, `around`, `over`, `cursor` or `self`), so the looks are free to animate the card's transforms and style it by where it went.
- While its card is open, a trigger has `is-open`.
- A trigger never breaks over two lines (`white-space: nowrap`), so it and its card are always one box to measure.
- A new page width closes the open card, since the text under it may re-flow.

Each variation is a look: a module in `js/variations/`, registered by its `data-variation` name in `js/index.js`, with its styles in `css/variations/<name>.css`, scoped by `[data-variation='<name>']`. A look has:

- `place` (optional): `'beside'`, `'around'`, `'over'`, `'cursor'`, `'below'` or `'self'`, as above.
- `hold` (optional): `true` for a card that holds itself open while the pointer is over it as well as over its trigger (it takes the pointer while open, and a tap inside it doesn't close it).
- `setup(section)` (optional): runs once, to add the elements it needs.
- `prepare(card, {term, block})` (optional): runs before the card is placed, to size it.
- `open(card, context)` and `close(card, context)`: each returns the tween or timeline it starts, so the next open or close can stop it. `context` is `{side, term, block, section, pointer}`.

With reduced motion each open and close jumps straight to its end (`progress(1)`), so every look shows its final state without moving.

What the looks share, written once:

- `js/hoverCards.js` exports, besides `GAP` and `isHidden`: `reducedMotion`; `urlOf(path)`, a path from the page (a picture's, in a data attribute) as a full URL, since a `url()` in a custom property resolves against the stylesheet using it; `loaded(image)`, a picture ready to draw, decoded or, when the browser turns decoding down (an `EncodingError`, which it can give for a picture that's fine), loaded, used by every look and drawing that reads a picture's pixels or size; `pageMargin()`; `linesFor(state, elements, key)`, text split into lines in masks (SplitText), split again only when what its wrapping depends on changes, keeping its no-break spaces (01 Backdrop, 05 Entry); `bounds(section)`, where a card can be up and down, on screen below the bar of numbers and, given its section, inside it; and `oneTweenEach()`, which keeps one tween at a time for each key (a trigger, a paragraph, a section), stopping the last when the next starts, since GSAP's `overwrite` doesn't stop tweens of CSS custom properties.
- `js/follow.js`: cards that come to the pointer and follow it (07 On Air, 11 Departures), and what 12 Scrub and 13 Rewind share as the pointer moves along their trigger: how far along it the pointer is (`along`, `alongTrigger`, which 15 Register uses too), their fades (`fadeIn`, `fadeOut`), and the trigger's underline as a tape (`tapeTo`, `emptyTape`, `keepTape`).
- `js/cuts.js`: cards cut into the paragraph itself (10 Doors, 14 Slats): where the cut goes (`cutFor`), the copy of the paragraph's text that faces it (`copyOf`), and the hand-over between overlapping cards (`handOver`, `closing`).
- `js/gl/program.js`: the WebGL drawings' program, positions and textures (04 Filmstrip, 06 Signal, 09 Instant, 16 Focus Pull).

## Triggers

A trigger looks one of three ways, set on its section with `data-trigger` (`css/base.css`). New variations use one of these three, nothing else. 09 Instant, 12 Scrub and 13 Rewind draw their underlines themselves, so they can move and fill: Scrub's and Rewind's (a tape, filling with the pointer) look like the others at rest, and Instant's is in its photo's paper colour, so the two read as one.

- `underline` (the default): the text's own colour, underlined 0.05em thick at 30% white, the line coming up to full on hover.
- `amber`: no underline, in the amber of the ground's glow, `rgb(var(--glow))`.
- `dim`: no underline, at 38% white (`--color-dim`), coming up to the text's colour (`--color-strong`) on hover.

## Type and spacing

One face, Host Grotesk, self-hosted in `fonts/` with its licence. The sizes hang from the display size, the show's name at 10vw (144px at 1440 wide): statements are a quarter of it (36px, line height ×1.15, −0.04em), with a floor for phones. The small type takes the sizes of the Codrops demo Text Character Animations: the frame and the labels at 13px (labels in sentence case, never capitals), and the cards' text at its small-text size, `--size-small`, 15px, 20px from 53em, line height ×1.3. All rounded to whole pixels. Statements, labels and the name are at weight 450; the frame is 13px at 400. Spacing is on a 15-point scale (15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240), with a 30px margin and gutter on a 12-column grid from 53em (15px below). A statement spans columns 2 to 7.

The ground (`css/base.css`) is fixed to the screen, a room at night lit only by a radio's dial. `--bg-layers` is a low pink glow rising from the bottom right (`--glow`) and a dark plum in the top left corner (`--plum`, from 3DStackMotion), on a greyish plum, `#473940` (`--color-bg`). Over both, `.static` (an empty element first in `body`) is a moving static: its `::before` tiles `img/static.png`, a seamless tile of soft, faintly coloured grain around mid grey, and `.static` blends it with `mix-blend-mode: overlay`, so it barely touches the black and comes up in the glow. The tile jumps between ten places 12 times a second (`@keyframes static`, `steps(1)`), so only a transform animates, and holds still with reduced motion; `.static` is the size of the screen and clips the tile's overhang, so the grain never shows past the page's edges. Its strength is `--static-opacity`. The elastic bounce past the top and bottom of the page is off (`overscroll-behavior: none`), since past its edges there's only the flat ground. The accent is `--color-accent: #e23e1d`.
